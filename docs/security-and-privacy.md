# Security and privacy

AI VAT Number Validation is built and hosted by iWebDevelopment, which is ISO 27001 certified.
Core infrastructure runs in the EU.

- The tool is read-only. Nothing can be created, changed or deleted through this connection.
- Connecting it gives us no access to any system of yours. There is no such connection to make.
- We do not keep a record of which VAT numbers you checked.
- Two external registers can be involved in one check: VIES, and for Dutch numbers the Dutch
  business register. Both are described in PRIVACY.md.
- The authoritative terms are in iWebDevelopment's
  [Algemene Voorwaarden (AV)](https://www.iwebdevelopment.com/hubfs/144641067/Files/algemene-voorwaarden.pdf)
  and the DPA.
